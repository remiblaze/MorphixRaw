# Morphix Raw: one-knob granular morph

![Morphix Raw free one-knob granular morph UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/MorphixRaw/main/morphixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob turns any sound into a shimmering granular cloud.**

Morphix Raw is the free, one-knob version of Morphix. Turn the single MORPH knob and your audio dissolves into a level-matched granular texture with vocal-formant character, with no sample loading. Just feed it audio and morph.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/MorphixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`MorphixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Morphix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`MorphixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Morphix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **MORPH** knob drives the whole granular engine at once. As you turn it up, the dry signal crossfades into a granular cloud, the vocal-formant morph deepens, pitch spray adds shimmer, and the texture is held at a consistent level so you can push it without the volume jumping. Works on anything: vocals, synths, drums, pads. That's the entire interface: the knob, the reactive Fire Head, and an About panel with links and version info.
