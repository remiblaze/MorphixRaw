# Morphix Raw — one-knob granular morph

![Morphix Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/MorphixRaw/main/morphixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob turns any sound into a shimmering granular cloud.**

Morphix Raw is the free, one-knob version of Morphix. Turn the single MORPH knob and your audio dissolves into a level-matched granular texture with vocal-formant character — no sample loading, just feed it audio and morph.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/MorphixRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`MorphixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **MORPH** knob drives the whole granular engine at once. As you turn it up, the dry signal crossfades into a granular cloud, the vocal-formant morph deepens, pitch spray adds shimmer, and the texture is held at a consistent level so you can push it without the volume jumping. Works on anything — vocals, synths, drums, pads. That's the entire interface: the knob, the reactive Fire Head, and an About panel with links and version info.
